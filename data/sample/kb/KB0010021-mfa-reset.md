# KB0010021: Reset MFA after changing your phone

Category: Access > Password/MFA · Assignment group: IAM-L1

Ask the HELIX bot "reset my MFA". You will receive an Okta Verify push or SSO re-authentication for step-up verification.
After verification your factors are cleared and an enrolment link is emailed. Enrol the new phone within 24 hours.
If you cannot complete step-up verification, contact the service desk with your employee ID.
