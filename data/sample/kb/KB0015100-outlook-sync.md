# KB0015100 · Outlook sync stuck

## Symptoms
Desktop Outlook shows "Trying to connect" and the Outbox does not clear.

## Checks
1. Confirm O365 webmail works.
2. Remove and re-add the account profile.
3. Verify VPN split-tunnel is not blocking Microsoft endpoints.

## Resolution
Reset the Outlook profile and re-sync. Escalate to Messaging Ops if the mailbox exceeds quota.
