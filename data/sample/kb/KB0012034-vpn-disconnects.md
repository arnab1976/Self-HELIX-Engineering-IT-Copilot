# KB0012034: GlobalProtect VPN disconnects every few minutes

Category: Access > VPN · Assignment group: Network-L2

## Symptoms
VPN drops every 5-15 minutes when working remotely, especially after the Monday client update.

## Resolution
1. Open GlobalProtect > Settings > Gateways and select the nearest gateway (Mumbai-2 for India).
2. Update the client to version 6.2.4 or later from the Software Centre.
3. If drops continue, collect logs (GlobalProtect > Troubleshooting > Collect Logs) and raise a ticket to Network-L2.
